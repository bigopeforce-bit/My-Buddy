# Web research

Use when the user asks a question that needs current facts, comparison or several sources.

1. Restate the question in one line, and what would count as a good answer.
2. Search with at least three different phrasings. Open three to five sources, preferring primary sources, official pages and recent dates.
3. For each source, note the date, who published it, and whether it is fact, estimate or opinion.
4. Look for disagreement. If sources conflict, say which is more credible and why.
5. Answer in this order:
   - the direct answer in one or two sentences
   - the evidence, with a link and date for each claim
   - what is still uncertain, and what would settle it
   - confidence: high, medium or low, with one reason
6. Never claim to have read a page that was blocked or paywalled. Say so and use another source.
7. Do not present a single blog post as consensus. If only one source says it, say that.
