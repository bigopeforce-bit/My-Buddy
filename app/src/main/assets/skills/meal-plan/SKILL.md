# Meal plan

Use when the user asks what to cook this week, wants a meal plan, or wants the shopping list that goes with it.

1. Start from what is there: ask what is in the fridge or list the staples the user mentions. Work with what exists.
2. Build a week: three or four dinners and one or two lunches. Keep the cooking effort even across the days.
3. Say for each meal what it needs, and mark the things that are missing from home.
4. Put the missing things in one shopping list, grouped by where they are bought (market, supermarket, shop).
5. Keep the plan short enough to read on a phone: one line per day.

## Rules
- Ask about allergies, guests and what the user does not eat before planning, when it is not already known.
- Do not add anything to a cart or order it; offer the list and ask first.
- Say plainly when a meal needs an oven, a long time or a pan the user may not have.
