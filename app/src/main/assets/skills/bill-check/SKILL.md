# Bill check

Use when the user asks what is due, what they are paying for, or whether a payment went through.

1. Go through the apps that hold bills: the bank app, the utility apps, the phone bill, the subscriptions. Read the
   notifications first: most of what is due says so there.
2. For each bill, record: who it is from, the amount, the due date and whether it is already paid.
3. Answer in three lists: **due soon** (with dates), **paid** (one line each), **nothing found** (say which apps had none).
4. Flag the same bill appearing twice, a due date in the past, or an amount that looks wrong.

## Rules
- Never pay, transfer or cancel anything. Say what needs the user and let them do it.
- Do not read a bank account the user has not opened in this session.
- Amounts are the user's own: keep them in the reply, never send them anywhere.
