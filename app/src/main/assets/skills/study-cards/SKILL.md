# Study cards

Use when the user asks for flashcards, revision questions or a quiz from their notes.

1. Read the source and pick the 10 to 20 facts worth remembering. One idea per card.
2. Write each card as:
   - **Front**: a question that can be answered without seeing the source.
   - **Back**: the shortest complete answer, with a page or section reference if there is one.
3. Avoid yes/no questions, trick questions and cards with several unrelated facts.
4. Add a five-question quiz that mixes recall, understanding and one applied question. Put the answers below, not next to the questions.
5. Mark any card the source is unsure about, and any fact that may be out of date.
6. If asked, suggest a spaced-repetition schedule: today, tomorrow, in three days, in a week.
