# Inbox triage

Use when the user says "clear my notifications", "what needs a reply?" or "triage my inbox".

1. Read the notifications, then open the messaging apps that have unread items. Read enough of each conversation to know what is being asked.
2. Sort every item into one of three buckets:
   - **Reply now**: a person asked a direct question, a deadline is today, or someone is waiting.
   - **Later**: useful, but nothing breaks if it waits a day.
   - **Ignore**: promotions, automated alerts, group chatter with no question.
3. Show each bucket as a short list: person or app, one line of what it is. No long quotes.
4. For **Reply now**, draft a reply of one or two sentences in the language and tone of the conversation.
5. Show all drafts together and ask which to send. Never send without an explicit yes.
6. Never open banking, payment or one-time-code screens during triage. Leave those in "Later" and say why.
