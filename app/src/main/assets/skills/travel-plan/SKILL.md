# Travel plan

Use when the user asks for an itinerary, a trip plan, or help deciding what to do in a city.

1. Ask for what is missing: dates, budget, pace (packed or relaxed), must-sees, who is travelling, and any mobility or food needs.
2. Check opening hours, seasonality and whether a booking is required. Do not assume a place is open.
3. Cluster each day by area so travel time stays short. Give travel time between stops, not just names.
4. For each day, write morning, afternoon and evening, plus one backup for rain or a closure.
5. Include costs in the local currency and in the user's currency, with the rate date.
6. End with:
   - what must be booked now, and by when
   - what can be decided on the day
   - one thing to avoid (crowds, closures, scams)
7. Do not book, reserve or pay for anything without an explicit approval of the exact booking and price.
