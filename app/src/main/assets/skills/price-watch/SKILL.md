# Price watch

Use when the user asks "is this a good price?", "find it cheaper", or wants a product compared across shops.

1. Pin down the exact item: model, size, colour, condition (new, refurbished, used) and region.
2. Check at least three sellers. For each, record:
   - item price and currency
   - shipping and any fees
   - delivery date
   - returns policy
   - seller type: official store, marketplace seller, unknown
3. Compare the landed total, not the sticker price. Note when a cheaper price needs a subscription or a bulk quantity.
4. Flag region locks, missing warranty, refurbished items sold as new, and prices that look too good.
5. Show a small table plus a recommendation: best total, best delivery, safest seller.
6. Do not add to a cart, start checkout, or buy anything. Ask the user first, and confirm the exact item and total.
7. If the user wants to watch the price, offer a scheduled check instead of checking repeatedly now.
