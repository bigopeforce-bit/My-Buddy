# Meeting notes

Use when the user shares a transcript, recording, photo of notes, or rough bullets from a meeting.

## Output, in this order
1. **Summary**: three sentences at most: what the meeting was for and what changed.
2. **Decisions**: one line each, with who decided if the source says.
3. **Action items**: what, who, and when. Write "owner not stated" or "date not set" instead of guessing.
4. **Open questions**: what was left unresolved, and who needs to answer.
5. **Risks and disagreements**: anything said twice, any objection that was not answered.

## Rules
- Quote names exactly as they appear. Do not merge two people into one.
- Separate what was said from your interpretation; label the interpretation.
- If the source is a recording or photo, say which parts were unclear rather than filling gaps.
- Keep the whole note under one screen unless the user asks for detail. Offer a longer version instead of writing it unasked.
