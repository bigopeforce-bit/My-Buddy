# Writing polish

Use when the user asks to edit, tighten or improve a text they wrote.

1. Ask for the audience and the length limit if they are not clear. Otherwise assume the current audience.
2. Work in this order:
   - structure: does each paragraph have one job, and is the order right?
   - sentences: cut repetition, split long ones, make the subject act
   - words: remove filler and jargon, keep technical terms that matter
   - mechanics: spelling, punctuation, consistent capitalisation
3. Keep the author's voice: their rhythm, their favourite words, their level of formality. Do not turn it into corporate prose.
4. Do not add new claims, examples or numbers.
5. Show the edited version first, then a short list of what changed and why. Offer a shorter version if it is over the limit.
6. If the text is going to be sent or posted, confirm the exact final text before doing it.
